### 🚨 Incident Management

**[INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PLAN.md](./INCIDENT-RESPONSE-PLAN.md)** - Security Incident Response
- Incident classification (P1-P4 severity)
- Response team and roles
- 7-phase response process:
  1. Detection (0-15 min)
  2. Classification (15-30 min)
  3. Containment (30 min - 2 hours)
  4. Investigation (2-24 hours)
  5. Eradication (4-12 hours)
  6. Recovery (12-72 hours)
  7. Post-Incident Review (within 7 days)
- Notification procedures (internal & external)
- Communication protocols
- Specific playbooks (data breach, ransomware, DDoS)
- Evidence collection
- Testing and drills

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Clear escalation paths defined
- ✅ 72-hour GDPR notification timeline
- ✅ 24/7 emergency hotline
- ✅ Quarterly tabletop exercises planned

**Audience:** Security team, incident responders, management
