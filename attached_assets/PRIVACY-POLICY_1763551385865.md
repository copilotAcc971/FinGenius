## 6. Your Privacy Rights

### 6.1 GDPR Rights (EU Users)

You have the right to:

**1. Access Your Data**
- Request a copy of all data we hold about you
- Delivered within 30 days, free of charge

**2. Rectify Your Data**
- Correct inaccurate information
- Update outdated information

**3. Erase Your Data ("Right to be Forgotten")**
- Request deletion of your data
- We will comply unless legal obligation to retain (e.g., tax records)

**4. Data Portability**
- Receive your data in machine-readable format (JSON/CSV)
- Transfer to another service provider

**5. Restrict Processing**
- Limit how we use your data
- Applies in specific circumstances

**6. Object to Processing**
- Object to processing based on legitimate interest
- Absolute right to object to marketing

**7. Human Review**
- Request human review of automated decisions
- Contest automated fraud detection decisions

### 6.2 California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

California residents have additional rights:
- Right to know what data is collected
- Right to delete personal information
- Right to opt-out of sale (we don't sell data)
- Right to non-discrimination for exercising rights

### 6.3 How to Exercise Your Rights

**Email:** privacy@company.com
**Portal:** Log in to your account > Settings > Privacy
**Mail:** [Company Address]

We will respond within 30 days (GDPR) or 45 days (CCPA).
